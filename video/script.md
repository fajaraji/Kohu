# Kohu demo video script (3:00)

Final render: `video/kohu-demo.mp4` (1280x720, 180 s). Every product shot is a real recording of the running app made with `video/capture.mjs`. Only the opening, the section cards and the closing are made with Hyperframes. Waiting time is sped up and labeled on screen with its factor. The narration is a synthetic voice (Kokoro TTS) and is labeled in the corner; the team may re-record it before upload.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:20 | Hyperframes opening. Three short statements, the 25% figure with its source line, then the Kohu name and the three Meta models. | Every company knows more than any one person can find. Know-how sits on a few laptops and in a few heads. Data the company paid for goes unopened. Our team has seen this firsthand. Atlassian found that teams lose a quarter of their time searching for answers. Kohu is one place to ask. |
| 0:20 to 1:00 | Card, then real footage. Employee asks how to process a customer refund. Waiting is sped up and labeled. The answer shows citation chips and a warning that two sources disagree. The page chip opens the process PDF page. The video chip plays the recording at the cited second, then the step frame with the SAM mark. | A new hire asks how to process a customer refund. Muse Spark searches only the sources this role may see, and every sentence comes back with a citation. This one opens the written process. This one plays the screen recording from the exact second, and SAM 3.1 marks the field to use. Kohu built these steps from the recording itself, with Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Spark. A reason is kept only when the narrator said it. |
| 1:00 to 1:20 | Card, then real footage. Employee asks which country grew fastest in 2024. The answer carries the licensed data notice. The page 4 chip opens the report chart with the SAM mark over it. | Next the new hire asks which country grew fastest last year. The answer comes from a report the company licensed for everyone, one most people never knew it had. SAM marks the chart, and Muse Spark reads the values. |
| 1:20 to 1:40 | Card, then real footage. Compare roles runs one question for all four roles. Employee gets the report figure. Analyst gets the dataset value with a cell citation, and the citation opens the spreadsheet row highlighted. | The full dataset behind that report has only a few seats. Asked by all four roles, only the analyst gets the exact value, cited to the row and cell. The others get nothing from it, and no hint that it exists. |
| 1:40 to 2:10 | Card, then real footage. Employee asks about Viet Nam 2023 and sees both versions. Steward opens Conflicts, writes a note, and marks the report as right. Employee asks again. The answer now gives 5.0 percent and shows the Steward decision. | Here two sources disagree. The licensed report says Viet Nam grew five percent in 2023. An internal slide says six and a half. Kohu shows both and does not pick. The steward, the data admin, decides that the report is right. Ask again, and the answer changes. The slide is marked as superseded. |
| 2:10 to 2:38 | Card, then real footage. Manager uploads a new screen recording. Processing time is sped up 24 times and labeled. The steps appear with frames, SAM marks and spoken reasons, and the manager approves them. | A manager uploads a new recording. Kohu transcribes it, sends the video and the transcript to Muse Spark, and gets back timed steps. The waiting is sped up here. Each step gets a frame, a mark from SAM, and a reason only if one was spoken. A person approves it before anyone can cite it. |
| 2:38 to 2:46 | Card, then real footage. License cards with vendor, coverage, seats, users in the last 30 days, and the sample data label. | The license card shows who used the paid data. The baseline is labeled sample data. |
| 2:46 to 3:00 | Hyperframes closing. Eval numbers with the caveat line, then the project name and repository. | We measured it. Nineteen of twenty frozen questions passed, with no leaks in seventy four access checks. The failures are in the repo too. |

Captions: `video/captions.srt` (burned into the render as well).
