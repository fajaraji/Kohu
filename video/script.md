# Kohu demo video script (2:09)

Final render: `video/kohu-demo.mp4` (1280x720, about 129 s), built by `video/build_video_v3.py`. Every product shot is a real recording of the running app made with `video/capture.mjs` on 2026-11-04. The upload segment uses a fresh recording that was not in the model cache, so it was processed live. The whole video is one Hyperframes composition in the app's dark style: the footage sits in an app frame, zooms in on what matters, and short transitions replace full-screen section cards. Waiting time is sped up and labeled on screen with its factor. The narration is a synthetic voice (Kokoro TTS) and is labeled in the corner; the team may re-record it before upload. The music is original, made by `video/make_music.py`, and ducks under the voice.
